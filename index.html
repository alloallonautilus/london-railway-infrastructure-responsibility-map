Data caveats

This is a prototype, not a verified current infrastructure map. OSM railway features, if loaded, are independently mapped and must not be assumed complete. The Network Rail GIS archive is explicitly marked out of date. Track geometry represents modelled centreline links; it is not an engineering or safety-critical representation.

The map uses the supplied Greater London boundary; archived route and region polygons and track links are clipped to it. The built-in Thames layer is extracted from a user-provided OSM export. It spans western, central and eastern reaches but has not passed a complete continuity check; gaps and bank mismatches may remain. Bundled NaPTAN RSE station markers are derived from station entrance coordinates; detailed OSM station features are requested uniformly for any viewed area. Leaflet is loaded online; no third-party basemap tiles are requested. The optional OSM infrastructure overlay makes a separate Overpass data request when you press Load.
